- **Lithium-Ion Battery Risk**: Lithium-ion batteries (such as 18650 cells, pouch cells, and TP4056 chargers) carry inherent risks of thermal runaway, severe fire, explosion, toxic fumes, and acid burns if shorted, overcharged, overheated, punctured, crushed, or improperly wired. The user is solely responsible for implementing adequate over-current, over-voltage, and thermal protection.

- **RF Power & Antenna Exposure**: RF power amplifiers can generate electromagnetic fields. Operators must maintain safe separation distances from transmitting antennas.
